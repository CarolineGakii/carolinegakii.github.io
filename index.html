About Me

Writer. Researcher. AI Professional.

I'm a Content & Technical Writer with 9 years of experience helping organizations communicate complex technology with clarity and confidence. I specialize in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, and software engineering.

Since 2020, I've partnered with international startups, SaaS companies, and AI organizations through Upwork — earning Top Rated status with consistent 5-star reviews and long-term client relationships. I've published 140+ articles on Medium, founded Mindful Data Science, and contributed to established publications including Good Vibes Good Life and Catharsis.

Alongside writing, I'm pursuing a Master's in Data Science at Meru University of Science and Technology. I evaluate frontier language models at Turing, and my Master's research focuses on AI-powered computer vision for medical diagnosis — combining ResNet50V2, EfficientNetV2, and CoAtNet for histopathology image classification.

I don't just write about AI. I build with it, evaluate it, and understand it at a technical level that most writers don't.
